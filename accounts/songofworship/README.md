# SongOfWorship Content Account

**Handle**: @songofworship (TikTok + Instagram)
**Product**: Personalized worship songs for life's biggest moments — songofworship.com

## Content Strategy

- **60% faith/devotional** — scripture carousels, worship life, prayer practices (audience builders)
- **25% gifting/occasions** — baptism, wedding, grief, milestones (conversion drivers)
- **15% behind-the-scenes** — song stories, songwriting process, the power of personalization

## CTA Strategy

- Faith content: organic CTAs ("save this for Sunday", "share with someone in a hard season")
- Gifting content: soft product mention ("personalized worship songs — link in bio")
- 80/20 rule: 80% value, 20% product-adjacent

## Key Principles (ported from DreamtimeLullabies)

- habit_list default format, 5 slides
- Specificity wins: "3 scriptures I texted a friend going through chemo" > "prayer is powerful"
- Actionable > inspirational
- Min hook score: 12/20
- Warm golden visual palette
